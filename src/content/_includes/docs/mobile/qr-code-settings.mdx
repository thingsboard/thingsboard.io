Configure your custom mobile app to launch directly from a <DocLink product={props.product} path="user-guide/ui/mobile-qr-code">QR code scan</DocLink> using the phone's camera.

<Aside type="note">
  This guide applies to app version 1.7.0 and later. For earlier versions, see the <DocLink product={props.product} path="qr-code-settings-before-v1.7">pre-1.7.x QR code configuration guide</DocLink>.
</Aside>

## iOS application setup

Navigate to **Mobile Center → Applications → Your custom iOS app** in the ThingsBoard platform.

### App Site Association ID

In your app store info settings, provide the **App Site Association ID**. It consists of two parts separated by a dot: `{Team ID}.{Bundle ID}`.

**Team ID** — find it in your [Apple Developer Account](https://developer.apple.com/account) under **Membership details**.

<DocImage
  src="/src/assets/images/mobile/qr-code-settings/qr-code-settings-team-id.png"
  alt="Team ID location in Apple Developer Account Membership details"
/>

**Bundle ID** — must match the Application package configured in your app settings.

## Android application setup

Navigate to **Mobile Center → Applications → Your custom Android app** in the ThingsBoard platform.

### SHA-256 fingerprint

Provide the **SHA-256 fingerprint** of your app's signing key.

{props.product === Products.MOBILE_PE ? (
  <DocImage
    src="/src/assets/images/mobile/qr-code-settings/pe/qr-code-settings-sha256.png"
    alt="SHA-256 fingerprint field in Android app settings"
  />
) : (
  <DocImage
    src="/src/assets/images/mobile/qr-code-settings/qr-code-settings-sha256.png"
    alt="SHA-256 fingerprint field in Android app settings"
  />
)}

- **Google Play distributed apps** — find the SHA-256 certificate fingerprint in the Google Play Console under **Release → Setup → App Integrity → App Signing** tab.

<DocImage
  src="/src/assets/images/mobile/qr-code-settings/sha256-fingerprint.png"
  alt="SHA-256 fingerprint location in Google Play Console App Signing tab"
/>

- **Locally distributed apps** — use the SHA-256 key that your APK is signed with.

For details, see [Set up app links for Android](https://docs.flutter.dev/cookbook/navigation/set-up-app-links) in the Flutter documentation.
