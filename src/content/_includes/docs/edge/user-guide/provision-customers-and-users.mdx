ThingsBoard Edge inherits user access from the ThingsBoard server automatically. Customers are created on the server, and users reach the Edge through the user groups assigned to it. On Edge 4.3 and later, a Tenant Administrator can also create users on the Edge, and the Edge pushes them to the server.

## User access model

Two types of users can log in to ThingsBoard Edge:

- **Tenant Administrator:** All Tenant Administrators on the ThingsBoard server can log in to any connected Edge instance without additional configuration. For what they can change on the Edge, see [Access permissions](#access-permissions).
- **Customer User:** A Customer User can log in to Edge only if the Edge instance has been assigned to their Customer account on the server. Once assigned, they can view only the entities that are assigned to both the Edge instance and their Customer account. An entity assigned only to the Edge, or only to the Customer, is not visible to Customer Users.

## Create a customer and user on the server

Before assigning an Edge instance to a customer, make sure the customer account and its users exist on the server.

<Steps>
1. Log in to the ThingsBoard server and go to **Customers & users > Customers**. Click **Add customer** (+) and enter the customer details.

2. Open the customer and click **Manage users**. Click **Add user** and enter the user's email address. On the **Owner and groups** step, select the user group.

3. Click **Add**. Depending on the **Activation method**, the dialog shows an activation link or sends an activation email. The user must activate their account before they can log in to Edge.
</Steps>

## Assign an Edge instance to a customer

Users are organized into user groups. To let a customer's users log in to an Edge, the Edge must belong to that customer, and their user group must be assigned to it:

<Steps>
1. On the ThingsBoard server, go to **Edge management > Edges** and click the Edge.
2. On the **Details** tab, click **Manage owner and groups**, select the customer as the owner, and click **Update**.
3. Go to the **Assignments** tab. In **User groups**, click **Assign user group to this edge**, select the customer's user group, and click **Assign**.
</Steps>

Users in the assigned group can now log in to the Edge and work with the entities in their group's scope. A customer's user group reaches only Edges owned by that customer. To give the group to several Edges of the customer at once, add it to their <DocLink product={props.product} path="user-guide/edge-management#edge-profiles">edge profile</DocLink>.

<Aside type="note">
If the Edge is offline when you assign a user group, the assignment waits in the queue and is applied once the connection is restored. Check the <DocLink product={props.product} path="user-guide/edge-status-events" target="_blank">Cloud events</DocLink> page on the Edge if user access does not appear after reconnecting.
</Aside>

## Access permissions

What each user type can do on a connected Edge:

| Role | On the Edge |
|------|-------------|
| Tenant Administrator | Creates and edits devices, assets, entity views, dashboards, and alarm rules. On Edge 4.0 and later, also edits rule chains. On Edge 4.3 and later, also creates users and scheduler events. Can read all other entities on the Edge. |
| Customer User | Reads the entities that are assigned both to the Edge and to their customer. |

<Aside type="note">
Customers and integration and converter templates are managed on the server. Integrations and converters are read-only on the Edge.
</Aside>

## Unassign a user group from Edge

Unassigning the user group from the Edge removes its users' access. Tenant Administrator access is not affected.

<Steps>
1. On the ThingsBoard server, open the Edge and go to its **Assignments** tab.
2. In **User groups**, find the group and click **Unassign from this edge**.
</Steps>

Users in that group can no longer log in to the Edge.
