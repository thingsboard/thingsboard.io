An entity view exposes a selected subset of a device's or asset's attributes and time series, so you can share that data without granting access to the full entity. For a general introduction, see <DocLink product={Products.PE} path="user-guide/entity-views" target="_blank">Entity views</DocLink>.

On ThingsBoard Edge, you can create entity views on the Edge or assign them from the server.

## Create an entity view on Edge

<Steps>
1. In the Edge UI, go to **Devices & assets > Entity views** and click **Add entity view**.

2. Enter a name, select the target entity, choose the attributes and time-series keys to expose, and click **Add**.
</Steps>

The entity view is pushed to the server automatically. To check, go to **Devices & assets > Entity views** on the server.

## Assign entity views from the server

Entity views reach an Edge through entity view groups:

<Steps>
1. On the ThingsBoard server, add the entity views to an entity view group.

2. Go to **Edge management > Edges** and click the Edge. On the **Details** tab, click **Manage entity view groups**.

3. Click **Assign to edge** (+), select the group, and click **Assign**.
</Steps>

The entity views of the group appear on the Edge under **Devices & assets > Entity views**. The target entity of an entity view must also be assigned to the Edge, for example through a device group, so that the entity view shows its data.

Unassigning the group removes its entity views from the Edge. They stay on the server.
