## v4.4.0 (Oct 5, 2026)

**Major** release with everything from [ThingsBoard v4.4.0](/docs/pe/releases/releases-table/v4-4-x/#v440-sep-29-2026).

Starting with this release, ThingsBoard Edge is one product, licensed under the Business Source License 1.1, and its Docker images and Linux packages use the `tb-edge` names: `thingsboard/tb-edge:<version>EDGE` and `tb-edge-<version>.deb` / `.rpm` from the [thingsboard-edge GitHub releases](https://github.com/thingsboard/thingsboard-edge/releases). Edge 4.4.0, 4.4.0.1 and 4.4.0.2 were also published under the former Edge PE names (`thingsboard/tb-edge-pe:<version>EDGEPE` and the `tb-edge-<version>pe` packages); those keep working, and the upgrade instructions use the `tb-edge` names.
