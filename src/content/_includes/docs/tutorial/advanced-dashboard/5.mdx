One of ThingsBoard's most powerful features is the ability to give customers access to their own data — and only their data — through a shared dashboard.
In this lesson, you will add two customers (office renters), assign ownership of devices and assets, and verify that each customer sees only their own premises.

<Aside type="note">
This lesson requires **ThingsBoard Professional Edition** or **ThingsBoard Cloud** for entity groups and role-based access control.
</Aside>

**Scenario:**
- **Customer A** rents Office A in Building A (devices: SD-001, EM-002, WM-003).
- **Customer B** rents Office B in Building A (device: AM-307).

---

## Step 5.1 Add Customers

<Steps>
1. Go to **Customers** (default group: **All**).
2. Click **+**, enter **Customer A**, and click **Add**.
3. Repeat to create **Customer B** and add them to the group **Office renters**.
</Steps>

---

## Step 5.2 Share the Dashboard

You cannot share an individual dashboard — only the group that contains it.

<Steps>
1. Go to **Dashboards → Groups** tab.
2. Click the **Share** icon next to the **Buildings** group.
3. Select **Customer A**, set permission to **Read**, and click **Share**.
4. Repeat for **Customer B**.
</Steps>

---

## Step 5.3 Assign Device Ownership

Assign Customer A as owner of the three devices in Office A:

<Steps>
1. Open device **SD-001** and click **Manage owner and groups**.
2. In the **Owner** row, select **Customer A**.
3. In **Groups**, type `Office A sensors` and click **Create a new one! → Add**.
4. Click **Update**.
5. Repeat for **EM-002** and **WM-003**.
</Steps>

Assign Customer B as owner of **AM-307**:

<Steps>
1. Open **AM-307** and click **Manage owner and groups**.
2. Set owner to **Customer B**, group to `Office B sensors` (create if needed).
3. Click **Update**.
</Steps>

---

## Step 5.4 Asset Access

### Assign Office Ownership

<Steps>
1. Open asset **Office A** and click **Manage owner and groups**.
2. Set owner to **Customer A**, group to `Customer A offices` (create if needed).
3. Click **Update**.
4. Repeat for **Office B** → **Customer B** → group `Customer B offices`.
</Steps>

### Share Building A

Both customers rent offices in Building A, so it must be shared with both.

<Steps>
1. Open asset **Building A** and click **Manage owner and groups**.
2. Create a new group named `Building A` and click **Update**.
3. Go to **Assets → Groups** tab.
4. Click the **Share** icon next to the **Building A** group.
5. Share with **Customer A** (Read) and **Customer B** (Read).
</Steps>

---

## Step 5.5 Create Customer Users

### Customer A — Emma Johnson

<Steps>
1. Go to **Customers**, open **Customer A**, and click **Manage customer users**.
2. Navigate to **Groups → Customer Users**.
3. Click **+**, enter email, first name (**Emma**), last name (**Johnson**), and click **Add**.
4. Copy the activation link and click **OK**.
5. Open the user, click the pencil icon, set the **Buildings** dashboard as the default, and enable **Always fullscreen**.
6. Save.
</Steps>

Repeat to create **Jack Smith** for Customer B.

---

## Step 5.6 Verify Customer Access

**Activate Emma Johnson:**

<Steps>
1. Paste the activation link into the browser, set a password, and log in.
2. The dashboard opens showing only Building A.
3. Click **Building A** to navigate to the building state — only Office A appears in the offices list.
4. Navigate to Office A and verify sensors SD-001, EM-002, and WM-003 are visible.
5. Open each device state and confirm telemetry is displayed correctly.
</Steps>

**Activate Jack Smith:**

Repeat the verification for Customer B — he should see only Office B and device AM-307.

---

## Conclusion

You have built a complete multi-level IoT dashboard from scratch:

1. Entities and hierarchical relationships
2. Multi-state dashboard with map and drill-down navigation
3. Simulated telemetry and per-device visualization states
4. Alarm rules with cross-hierarchy propagation
5. Scoped customer access — each customer sees only their own data

From here, you can extend the dashboard with additional device types, custom widgets, or automated notifications.
