Trendz operates as an add-on inside ThingsBoard. Activation is handled through the ThingsBoard billing or licensing interface — the exact flow depends on how your ThingsBoard environment is hosted.

Choose the guide that matches your setup:

- **<DocLink product={props.product} path="trendz/activation/public-cloud">ThingsBoard Cloud (US / EU)</DocLink>** — activate via the Plan and Billing page in your cloud tenant.
- **<DocLink product={props.product} path="trendz/activation/private-cloud">ThingsBoard Private Cloud</DocLink>** — a fully managed, isolated cluster; contact us to get started.
- **<DocLink product={props.product} path="trendz/activation/self-managed">ThingsBoard Self-Managed</DocLink>** — activate via the ThingsBoard License Portal.
