**Vibration analysis** catches machine problems from the way a machine shakes, before they turn into a
breakdown. Pumps, fans, motors, compressors, and gearboxes all change their vibration when something starts to
go wrong, and they do it weeks or months before you can hear it, feel the heat, or see it in the output.

Trendz turns the vibration sensors you already connect to ThingsBoard into an online condition monitoring
system. It reads the raw vibration signal of every machine and tells you three things about it: whether it has
changed, how serious that is, and what the most likely cause is. Any wired or wireless accelerometer that sends
raw waveform samples to ThingsBoard, directly or through a gateway, can be analyzed.

Acting on that early signal instead of waiting for the breakdown is what predictive maintenance means, and
vibration analysis in Trendz implements it for your machines.

## Predictive maintenance

Predictive maintenance services a machine when its condition calls for it, instead of after a failure or on a
fixed calendar. There are three ways to look after the same machine:

- **Reactive:** Run it until it fails, then repair it. Every fault becomes unplanned downtime.
- **Preventive:** Service it at fixed intervals. Healthy machines are opened for nothing, and worn ones still
  fail between services.
- **Predictive:** Watch its condition and act when it changes. Repairs move into planned downtime, parts are
  ordered ahead, and healthy machines are left alone.

<ImageGallery images={[

    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/vibration/maintenance-strategies.svg',

Predictive maintenance is a cycle: measure regularly, learn what is normal for each machine, detect a change,
diagnose the cause, plan the repair, and verify the result. Trendz covers every step except the planning and the
repair itself, and hands its findings to ThingsBoard alarms, rule chains, and notifications.

## How it works in Trendz

A vibration model connects the sensors to the alarms. Once it is set up, every scan runs on its own.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/vibration/vibration-blueprint.svg',
    alt: 'Blueprint of a vibration model across ThingsBoard and Trendz: a sensor sends a 3-second waveform to device telemetry, which ThingsBoard stores in its database; a scheduled scan starts a scan task that reads new samples, analyzes them, saves results to the Trendz database, saves metrics back to device telemetry, and raises alarms, which ThingsBoard stores and passes to dashboards and rule chains',
    caption: 'One vibration sample, from the sensor to an alarm. Follow the numbers from 1 to 9.',
  },
]} />

1. **Send a sample:** The sensor records a 3-second waveform, for example once a day, and sends it to ThingsBoard.
   The format is described in <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/getting-started'>Getting started</DocLink>.
2. **Store it:** ThingsBoard stores the waveform as device telemetry.
3. **Start a scan:** On the <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/monitoring#set-up-continuous-monitoring'>schedule set on the model</DocLink>, for example every 6 hours, Trendz starts a
   <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/interface/workspace#tasks-tab'>scan task</DocLink>.
4. **Read new samples:** The scan reads the newest sample of each device.
5. **Analyze:** Trendz computes the spectra, the metrics, the ISO zone, and the fault scores of each sample.
6. **Save to the database:** Trendz stores the spectra and results in its own database, for the
   <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/interface/analyzer-tab'>Analyzer</DocLink>.
7. **Save metrics:** Trendz writes RMS, peak-to-peak, and other metrics back to ThingsBoard as
   <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/monitoring#save-metrics-as-telemetry'>telemetry</DocLink>.
8. **Raise alarms:** Trendz checks every alarm rule on the new sample. A breached rule raises an alarm, which
   ThingsBoard stores. See <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/monitoring'>Monitoring and alarms</DocLink>.
9. **Act on it:** Dashboards show the metrics and alarms, and rule chains send them on by email, SMS, or to a
   ticketing system.

machine when you want to see the evidence yourself.
