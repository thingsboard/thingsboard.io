The **Monitoring** tab answers whether a machine is getting worse, and since when. The
<DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/interface/overview-tab'>Overview tab</DocLink> grades the latest sample; this tab follows each machine over time against its own median and against the alarm rules of the
model, and lists every alarm the rules have raised.

Open it when an alarm arrives in ThingsBoard, to see which series broke which rule and how long it has been open,
or to check that the schedule, the telemetry, and the alarms are running for the whole fleet.

## Layout

Pick a machine in the items table, and every card above it switches to that machine.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/vibration/monitoring-tab-layout.webp',
    alt: 'Monitoring tab of the Conveyor drives vibration model with six numbered areas: alarm state, series tiles, trend chart, Monitoring card, Alarms card, and items table',
    caption: 'Monitoring tab of Conveyor 4E: its alarm state, its series, and the fleet below.',
  },
]} />

| # | Area | Contents |
|---|------|---------------|
| **1** | **[Alarm state](#alarm-state)** | The worst alarm open on the selected machine |
| **2** | **[Series tiles](#series-tiles)** | The latest value of each metric or band, against the machine's median |
| **3** | **[Trend chart](#trend-chart)** | The selected series over time, with its median |
| **4** | **[Monitoring card](#monitoring-card)** | The state of continuous monitoring for the model |
| **5** | **[Alarms card](#alarms-card)** | The alarm episodes of the selected machine |
| **6** | **[Items table](#items-table)** | Every machine with its ISO zone and alarms |

<Aside>
This tab reports on the alarm rules of the model. How to create them is described in
<DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/monitoring#alarm-rules'>Monitoring & Alarms</DocLink>.
</Aside>

## Components

### Alarm state

The card at the top left names the selected machine and its alarm state:

- **With an alarm:** the worst alarm open on the machine, its severity, and how many of the model's rules are
  firing.
- **Without an alarm:** **No alarms raised** and **CLEAR**. The rules are checked, and nothing breaks them.

### Series tiles

The tiles list the latest value of each series, its alarm rule, and how far it sits above the machine's own
median. A tile whose rule is firing takes the severity color.

The tiles and the chart show one of two groups. **Metrics** shows RMS, acceleration RMS, peak-to-peak, and crest
factor. **Bands** shows the five frequency bands around the shaft speed. Use the switch at the top of the tiles to
switch between them.

### Trend chart

The chart draws the selected series on every sample in the time range, with the machine's median dashed.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {

    caption: 'Trend chart: velocity RMS of Conveyor 4E, with its median dashed.',

]} />

Use the <DocLink product={props.product} path="date-picker">date picker</DocLink> to change the time range, or **Group by** to plot one point per minute, hour, day, week,
month, quarter, or year instead of every sample. Each point shows the highest value in its period.

### Alarms card

The alarm episodes of the selected machine: the **Rule** that fired, its **Severity**, and when it **Opened**.
Sort by any column, and page through with the arrows at the bottom. With nothing open, the card reads
**Everything OK**.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/vibration/monitoring-alarms-card.webp',
    alt: 'Alarms card of Conveyor 4E listing four open alarms: ISO zone, Bearing defect on outer race, and Velocity RMS over limit at Critical, and Acceleration RMS above median at Major',
    caption: 'The Alarms card: four episodes open on Conveyor 4E.',
  },
]} />

Click an alarm to see it on the chart. The chart shades the time the alarm has been open, in its severity color,
and switches to what the rule watches:

| Rule | The chart shows |
|------|-----------------|
| **Reading over a limit** on a metric or a band | That metric or band, with a line for each severity level it crossed |
| **Reading over a limit** on **Any metric** or **Any band** | Every series of the category, each with its own levels |
| **ISO zone** | Velocity RMS, the reading the ISO zones are graded on, with the zone borders |

Shaft fault and bearing defect alarms are not drawn on the chart; open the sample in the
<DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/interface/analyzer-tab'>Analyzer tab</DocLink> to see the fault
frequencies. Click the alarm again to clear the chart.

### Items table

Every machine of the model. Click a row to switch the cards above to that machine.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/vibration/monitoring-items.webp',
    alt: 'Monitoring tab with the items table highlighted: five conveyors with their ISO zone, open alarms, severity, alarm rule, coverage, last scan, and scan status',
    caption: 'The fleet at a glance: Conveyor 4E is in zone D with a Critical alarm.',
  },
]} />

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Item** | The machine |
| **ISO zone** | The zone of its latest sample |
| **Alarms** | How many episodes are open on it |
| **Severity** | The worst of them |
| **Alarm rule** | The rule behind that severity, with a count of the others |
| **Coverage** | Samples stored for this machine, and the span they cover |
| **Last scan** | When a scan last covered it |
| **Scan status** | Where it stands in the current or last scan |

The **...** menu at the end of a row works on that one machine:

- **Scan latest data** and **Scan more data** scan only this machine. How both scans work is described in
  <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/interface/workspace#actions'>Actions</DocLink>.
- **Delete** removes the machine from the model, with its samples, spectra, and alarms. You can add it back on the
  <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/interface/properties-tab#entity-selection'>Properties tab</DocLink>.
