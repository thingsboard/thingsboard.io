The **Overview** tab answers the first question about a machine: is it healthy, and if not, what is wrong with it.
It gives the verdicts on the latest sample and shows how the machine degrades over time.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/vibration/interface-overview.webp',
    alt: 'Overview tab showing the item status card, shaft fault scores, the view switch with its chart, the monitoring card, the evidence panel, and the items table',
    caption: 'Overview tab: the health of one machine at the top, all machines of the model below.',
  },
]} />

Open it after a scan to see which machines are in trouble, or to pick the machine to study in the Analyzer.
Click a machine in the items table, and every card above shows that machine.

| # | Area | Contents |

| **1** | **[Item status card](#item-status-card)** | Three results for the selected machine: its ISO zone, its most likely shaft problem, and its most likely bearing problem |
| **2** | **[Shaft fault scores](#shaft-fault-scores)** | How likely each fault is, scored from how the frequency bands grew against the machine's median |
| **3** | **[View switch](#switch)** | Decides which view the chart and the panel show: Vibration level, Shaft faults, or Bearing faults |
| **4** | **[Chart](#views)** | How the values of the selected view changed over time |
| **5** | **[Monitoring card](#monitoring-card)** | The state of continuous monitoring for the model |
| **6** | **[View panel](#views)** | Details of the selected view, next to its chart. What it lists depends on the view |
| **7** | **[Items table](#items-table)** | All machines of the model, with the results of their latest sample |

## Components

### Item status card

The card at the top left shows the name of the selected machine and three results from its latest sample:

| Result | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **ISO** | The ISO 10816-1 zone, from A (good) to D (danger). Next to it: the ISO machine class used, and the velocity RMS the zone is based on |
| **Shaft problem** | The most likely shaft problem, such as unbalance or looseness. Next to it: the frequency band that points to it, and how many times that band has grown |
| **Bearing defect** | The strongest bearing fault frequency found, the bearing it belongs to, and its frequency in Hz |

### Shaft fault scores

The card below scores each fault by how the frequency bands of this machine have grown against its own median.
One row per fault:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Fault** | Unbalance, Misalignment, Looseness, or Bearing defect |
| **Verdict** | **Not seen**, **Probable**, or **Most probable**. **Most probable** marks the fault with the highest score |
| **Band** | The frequency band the score is based on, and how many times it has grown. The bearing row names what was found instead, such as a series of harmonics |
| **Score** | The score of the fault. At 1.00 or higher, the fault counts as present |

What each fault looks like in the bands is described in
<DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/basics#fault-signatures-unbalance-misalignment-looseness-and-bearing-damage'>Introduction to vibration analysis</DocLink>.

### Items table

The table at the bottom lists all machines of the model, the most recently scanned first. Click a row to select
that machine, and the cards, charts, and panels above show it.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Item** | The machine |
| **ISO zone** | The zone of the latest sample: A good, B acceptable, C alert, D danger |
| **Most likely shaft problem** | The shaft problem with the highest score, or a dash when no problem is found |
| **Most likely bearing problem** | The strongest bearing fault frequency found, with its bearing, its amplitude, and its frequency |
| **Latest vibration sample** | When the newest stored sample was recorded |
| **Last scanned** | When a scan was last launched for this machine |
| **Scan status** | The state of this machine in the last scan |
| **Actions** | The row menu: **Scan latest data**, **Scan more data**, **Delete** |

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/vibration/interface-overview-items.webp',
    alt: 'Overview tab with the items table highlighted, listing five machines with their ISO zone, shaft problem, bearing problem, latest sample, last scan, and scan status',
    caption: 'Items table: all machines of the model, with the results of their latest sample.',
  },
]} />

**Scan status** shows the state of each machine in the last scan:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Queued** | The scan has started, but has not reached this machine yet |
| **Computing** | The machine is being scanned now. The counter shows how many samples are done |
| **Computed** | The scan of this machine finished, and its results are shown on the tabs |
| **Failed** | The scan of this machine failed. Hover the status to see why |
| **Cancelled** | The scan was stopped before it reached this machine |
| **Interrupted** | The scan ended before it finished, for example because the server restarted |

The **...** menu at the end of a row works on that one machine:

- **Scan latest data** and **Scan more data** scan only this machine. How both scans work is described in
  <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/interface/workspace#actions'>Actions</DocLink>.
- **Delete** removes the machine from the model, with its samples, spectra, and alarms. You can add it back on the
  <DocLink product={props.product} path='vibration/interface/properties-tab#entity-selection'>Properties tab</DocLink>.

## Views

### Switch

The view switch decides what the chart and the panel show for the selected machine:

| View | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Vibration level** | The overall vibration metrics, such as velocity RMS, compared with this machine's usual level |
| **Shaft faults** | The frequency bands that show unbalance, misalignment, and looseness |
| **Bearing faults** | The bearing fault frequencies in the envelope spectrum, and how they grow over time |

Each button shows how many times the worst value in that view is above the machine's usual level, colored by
severity. You can see which view needs attention before you open it.

### Vibration level view

The view has two blocks: the metric trend on the left and the metrics panel on the right.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {

    caption: 'Vibration level view: the metrics over time, and their latest values.',

**Metric trend.** One chart row per metric, with every sample in the time window. The dashed line is the
machine's usual level, its median. Drag the bar under the chart, or scroll over the chart, to zoom into part of the
time window.

**Metrics panel.** The value of each metric in the latest sample, and how many times higher or lower it is than
the median.

**Show one metric.** Click a row in the panel to show only that metric, with its own scale. For velocity RMS,
the chart also draws the ISO zone borders. Click the same row again to show all four metrics.

### Shaft faults view

The view has two blocks: the band levels on the left and the frequency bands panel on the right.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/vibration/interface-shaft-view.webp',
    alt: 'Shaft faults view of the Overview tab with the band levels chart and the frequency bands panel highlighted together',
    caption: 'Shaft faults view: the bands over time, and their latest levels.',
  },
]} />

**Band levels.** One chart row per band, with every sample in the time window. The dashed line is the machine's
usual level, its median. The five bands are ranges in multiples of the shaft speed: 0.1 to 0.8× (sub-harmonic),
1×, 2×, 2.5 to 10× (harmonics), and above 10× (non-synchronous).

**Frequency bands panel.** The level of each band in the latest sample, and how many times higher or lower it is
than the median.

**Show one band.** Click a row in the panel to show only that band, with its own scale. Click the same row again
to show all five bands.

### Bearing faults view

The view has three blocks: the envelope spectrum and the frequency history on the left, and the strongest
frequencies panel on the right.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/vibration/interface-envelope-view.webp',
    alt: 'Bearing faults view of the Overview tab with the envelope spectrum, the line history, and the top lines panel highlighted together',
    caption: 'Bearing faults view: the envelope spectrum, the history of one frequency, and the strongest frequencies.',
  },
]} />

**Envelope spectrum.** The envelope spectrum of the latest sample. The frequency selected in the panel and its
harmonics are marked on it.

**Frequency history.** The amplitude of the selected frequency in every sample of the time window. The dashed line
is the machine's usual level, its median.

**Strongest frequencies panel.** The strongest frequencies of the latest sample, the strongest first, with how many
times each is above the median. A frequency is named when it matches a bearing fault frequency or a multiple of the
shaft speed, and shows **(unlabelled)** otherwise.

**Show one frequency.** Click a row in the panel to mark that frequency and its harmonics on the spectrum, and to
show its history below. Click another row to switch to it.
