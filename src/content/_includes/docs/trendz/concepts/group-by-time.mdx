To show how data changes over time, put the **Date** field on the **X-axis** of a view. Trendz splits the time range
of the <DocLink product={props.product} path="date-picker">date picker</DocLink> into intervals, called buckets, and
reduces the data in each bucket to one value. A bucket without data is shown as `-`.

## How grouping works

Each bucket covers one interval of the range. The field on the Y-axis is aggregated inside it, for example summed. A
smaller bucket gives more points and more detail.

<ImageGallery images={[
  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/group-by-time/group-by-buckets.svg',
    alt: 'One week of hourly readings grouped by day into 7 points, by 6 hours into 28 points, and by hour into 168 points',
    caption: 'One week of hourly readings in 7, 28, or 168 buckets.',

]} />

The Date field has two kinds of grouping:

| Grouping | Bucket size is set by | Use it for |
|----------|-----------------------|------------|
| **[RAW](#raw-mode)** | The **group by** control, at any time | Trends you zoom in and out of |
| **[Fixed option](#fixed-aggregation-options)** | The view configuration | Patterns, like the typical day or week |

## RAW mode

**RAW** is the default Date aggregation. The **group by** control next to the date picker sets the bucket size, and
anyone who opens the view can change it.

| Unit | One bucket per |
|------|----------------|
| minute | Minute |
| hour | Hour |
| day | Calendar day |
| week | Calendar week, starting on Monday |
| month | Calendar month |
| quarter | Calendar quarter |
| year | Calendar year |

## Group by amount

**Group by amount** merges several units into one bucket, like every 6 hours or every 3 days. It works in RAW mode
only.

<Steps>
1. Open the view settings and select **Group by amount** in the **General** section.

2. An amount field appears in the **group by** control, before the unit. Enter the number of units per bucket.

3. Click **Build report**.
</Steps>

## Fixed aggregation options

A fixed option groups by a part of the date, like the hour of the day or the day of the week, and ignores the rest.
Readings from every day in the range with the same hour land in the same bucket, so the chart shows a pattern
whatever the range.

  {
    src: '/src/assets/images/trendz/group-by-time/group-by-fixed.svg',
    alt: 'Hourly buckets of five days folded by the HOUR option into 24 buckets, one per hour of the day',
    caption: 'HOUR folds every day of the range into 24 buckets.',
  },
]} />

<Steps>
1. Click the **Date** field on the **X-axis**.

2. In **Aggregation**, select an option instead of **RAW**.

3. Click **Build report**.
</Steps>

With a fixed option the **group by** control is hidden.

| Option | Groups data by | Example label |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| **MINUTE** | Minute of the hour (0–59) | `45` |
| **HOUR** | Hour of the day (0–23) | `23` |
| **DAY** | Day of the week (Mon–Sun) | `Monday` |
| **DATE** | Day of the month (1–31) | `15` |
| **FULL_MINUTE** | Exact date and minute | `2026-03-01 23:45` |
| **FULL_HOUR** | Exact date and hour | `2026-03-01 23` |
| **FULL_DATE** | Exact calendar date | `2026-03-01` |
| **FULL_QUARTER** | Year and quarter | `2026-Q1` |
| **START_OF_WEEK** | Monday of the calendar week | `2026-03-02` |
| **WEEK_OF_YEAR** | Week of the year (W1–W54) | `W10` |
| **WEEK_OF_MONTH** | Week of the month (1–6) | `2` |
| **MONTH** | Month of the year (Jan–Dec) | `March` |
| **YEARMONTH** | Year and month | `2026-Feb` |
| **QUARTER** | Quarter of the year (1–4) | `1` |
| **YEAR** | Calendar year | `2026` |

The **FULL_** options, **START_OF_WEEK**, **YEARMONTH**, and **YEAR** keep the date, so they give a timeline with a
fixed bucket size. The others fold the range into a pattern.

Day and week boundaries follow the time zone of the
<DocLink product={props.product} path="date-picker#change-the-time-zone">date picker</DocLink>. **START_OF_WEEK** and
**WEEK_OF_YEAR** label a bucket by its week even when Monday is outside the range; only data inside the range is
counted.

## RAW or fixed

| Choose | When |
|--------|------|
| **RAW** | The view is used with different ranges, and viewers change the detail with **group by** |
| **Fixed option** | You want a pattern, like average energy per hour of the day or per day of the week |
