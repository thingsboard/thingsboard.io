## The model field

A model field holds the forecast of a prediction model, for every item of the model. You use it in a view like any
other field of the business entity.

- **When it is created:** Trendz adds the field when you turn on **Periodic refresh** for the model, as described
  in <DocLink product={props.product} path="prediction/monitoring#set-up-continuous-monitoring">Monitoring & Alarms</DocLink>.
  When you turn it off, the field is removed. When you turn it on again, the same field comes back, so saved views
  keep working.
- **Its name:** the name of the model, here **Site energy peak alert**. When you rename the model, the field is
  renamed too.
- **Where to find it:** in the field list of the view editor, under the business entity the model learns from,
  here **Site Energy**. Model fields are marked with a trend icon.

## Build a prediction view

The steps below build one example: a line chart of the measured energy and its forecast, shared on a dashboard.
The model field works in any Trendz view, like any other field, so you can also put it in a table, a card, or a
bar chart, and combine it with other fields.

### Step 1. Create a line view

1. On the Trendz home page, click **Create View**.
2. Choose **Line**. The view editor opens.

### Step 2. Add the fields

1. Drag **Date** from the field list to **X Axis**.
2. Open **Site Energy** and drag the model field, **Site energy peak alert**, to **Y Axis**.
3. Drag the telemetry the model predicts, **total**, to **Y Axis** as well.

On **Y Axis**, the measured telemetry must be above the model field, as in the third image. A field you drop lands at
the top of the list, so drag the model field first and the telemetry second. Both use the same aggregation, **SUM**
here, so the forecast continues the measured line.

### Step 3. Set the filter and period

1. Drag the entity itself, **Site Energy**, to **Filters**.
2. Click the filter and pick one item, **site-energy-001** here.
3. Click the period and choose one that reaches into the future, as far as the forecast goes.
4. Click **group by** and choose the same step as the model, **hour** here.

Click **Build report**. The chart shows the measured values up to the latest reading, and the forecast after it as

Click the check mark, choose the collection to save the view in, and click **Save**. A view has to be saved before
it can be shared.

## Prediction field settings

Click the model field on **Y Axis** to open its settings. **Telemetry source** chooses where the forecast is read
from:

- **Product:** the forecast stored in Trendz.
- **Platform:** the forecast saved to ThingsBoard. It needs **Save telemetry** on the model.
