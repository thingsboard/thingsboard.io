This guide builds your first prediction model: a forecast of the energy each site will use over the next 7 days.
It takes a few minutes and no code. Trendz learns from the readings your devices already sent to ThingsBoard.

By the end of this guide, you will have this model:

**Before you start:** you need devices of one profile in ThingsBoard that send a number regularly, for example hourly
energy, with a few weeks of history.

## Create your first model

### Step 1. Open the wizard

Go to **Prediction models** in the left menu and click **Create model**. Then pick what you want to predict. For a
first model, choose **Forecast** and click **Next**.

### Step 2. Choose the data

Tell Trendz which devices to learn from and which reading to predict:

1. Pick the device profile, here **Site Energy**.
2. Pick the reading to predict, here **total**, the energy used.
3. Check the **Data Readiness** panel on the right. **Good** or **Excellent** means there is enough history.

Click **Next**.

### Step 3. Choose how far ahead, and build

Pick how far into the future to predict, for example **Next 7 days**. Leave everything else as it is, and click
**Build model**. Trendz trains the model, which takes from a few seconds to a few minutes.

The other options are already set to the values Trendz recommends, so you get the best forecast possible from the
data you selected without changing anything.

### Step 4. See the forecast

The model page opens when training is done. The chart shows the measured readings as a solid line and the forecast
as a dashed line after **Now**. The table below lists every device with its latest reading and its next predicted
value.

### Step 5. Keep it up to date

A new model predicts once. To refresh the forecast as new readings arrive, click **Monitoring** in the page header:

1. Switch **Periodic refresh** on and choose how often, for example every hour.
2. Switch **Scheduled retrain** on. The model then relearns from the newest readings before each forecast, so it
   follows changes in how your devices behave.
3. Optionally switch **Save telemetry** on and enter a **Telemetry key**, so the forecast is also saved in
   ThingsBoard for your dashboards.
4. Click **Save changes**.
