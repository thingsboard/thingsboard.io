The **Subscription** tab in <DocLink product={props.product} path='user-guide/billing-info'>Plan and billing</DocLink> shows your current plan and how much of each plan limit you use. From this tab you change the plan, turn on add-ons, buy top-ups, apply a coupon, or cancel the subscription.

For plan prices, limits, and top-up contents, see <DocLink product={props.product} path='reference/subscriptions'>Subscription Plans</DocLink>.

## Management

The **Management** card shows:

- **Subscription plan** — the plan name and its status, for example **Active**;
- **Subscription period** — the start and end dates of the current billing period;
- **Next charge** — the amount and the date of the next payment (paid plans).

Buttons on the right: **Update plan**, **Cancel subscription**, and, on paid plans, **Apply coupon**.

If the latest invoice is unpaid, **Pay latest invoice** replaces **Update plan**, and **Apply coupon** is hidden.

## Subscription Plan Data

The **Subscription plan data** card has two views.

**Entities & Features** shows:

- the add-ons: **White-labeling feature**, **Edge Computing**, and **Trendz Analytics**. An add-on that is on shows **Enabled**; one that is off shows **Add to plan** (**Choose Pilot plan** for **White-labeling feature**). With **Edge Computing** on, an **Edges** row shows how many edge instances you use;
- entity usage against the plan limit: **Devices**, **Assets**, **Agents**, **Customers**, **Users**, **Dashboards**, **Rule chains**, **Integrations**, **Converters**, and **Scheduler events**;
- **Default storage TTL days** and **Calculated fields per entity**.

**API calls** shows monthly usage: **Transport messages**, **Transport data points**, **Data points storage days**, **Rule engine executions**, **Java script function executions**, **Emails**, **SMS**, **Alarms created**, **Reports created**, and **AI credits**. Click **See details** to open the **API usage** page with each limit and hourly activity charts.

Click **+** next to a limit to open **Update plan** at the **Customize** step and add a top-up. Limits without **+** cannot be topped up.

## Update Plan

Click **Update plan** to open a three-step wizard. You can also open it from the **Home** page: click **Upgrade** in the **Usage** widget.

<Steps>
1. **Choose the Subscription Plan.** The plans are **Free**, **Prototype**, **Pilot**, **Startup**, and **Business**. Each card shows the monthly price, the number of devices and assets, the data points per month, and **White labeling** for Pilot and higher. Your plan is marked **Current plan**. Click **Select** on another plan, or **Customize** on your current plan. The wizard moves to the **Customize** step.

   <Aside type="note">
     Need a dedicated deployment? Click **See more** under **Thinking about migrating to Private Cloud?** to learn about ThingsBoard Private Cloud.
   </Aside>

2. **Customize.** Choose add-ons on the left and top-ups on the right. The total per month updates as you change them.

   **Add-ons** (turn on the toggle to add one):
   - <DocLink product={props.product} path='user-guide/white-labeling/' target="_blank">White Labeling</DocLink> — your branding in the UI. Included from the Pilot plan and always on.
   - <DocLink product={props.product} path='user-guide/edge-computing/' target="_blank">Edge Computing</DocLink> — Edge instances at remote sites for offline operation and local processing, synced with the cloud. The card shows how many instances are included; set the total in **Edge Instances**. Each additional instance is charged separately.
   - <DocLink product={props.product} path='user-guide/trendz-analytics/' target="_blank">Trendz Analytics</DocLink> — advanced analytics, custom dashboards, and trend discovery. The card shows how many devices are included.

   **Top-ups** have two views:
   - **Entities** — **Extra Device Pack**, **Extra Customer Pack**, **Extra Integration Pack**, **Extra Calculated Field**, and **Extra Remote Agent**;
   - **API calls** — **Traffic Pack**, **Compute Pack**, **Storage Pack**, **Alarm Pack**, **Email Pack**, **SMS Pack**, and **AI Credits Pack**.

   Use **−** and **+** to set the number of packs. Each limit shows how much it grows, for example **+10**, and the view toggle shows the cost of the selected packs. If a pack is not available on your plan, a button such as **Update to Startup plan** appears instead.

   On plans below Pilot, for example Prototype, top-ups and White Labeling are locked: the step shows the banner *White labeling and Top-ups are available starting from Pilot plan.* with **Update to Pilot**, and the **White Labeling** card shows **Unlock with Pilot plan** instead of a toggle.

   Click **Next: Summary**.

3. **Summary.** Check the price breakdown:
   - for each add-on: the base price, what is included (for example, **Included Edges** or **Included Devices**), and the cost of extras;
   - for each top-up: **Number of Packs** and **Pack price**;
   - **Final Add-ons price**, **Final Top-ups price**, and the new **Total** per month;
   - **Additional charge after plan update** — what you pay now for the rest of the current billing period.

   Click **Update plan** to confirm, or **Back to add-ons** to change the selection.
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
  Payments are non-refundable. Check the plan, add-ons, and top-ups before you click **Update plan**.
</Aside>

To apply a coupon while you change the plan, click **Apply coupon** next to **Total** in the **Customize** or **Summary** step.

## Apply a Coupon

<Steps>
1. On a paid plan, click **Apply coupon** in the **Management** card.
2. Enter the **Coupon code** and click **Apply**.
3. Click **Save**.
</Steps>

## Cancel the Subscription

<Aside type="danger">
  Canceling permanently deletes your tenant account, all devices, assets, dashboards, rule chains, users, and other entities, and all telemetry data and alarms. This action cannot be undone.
</Aside>

<Steps>
1. Click **Cancel subscription** in the **Management** card.
2. Select a reason in **Why are you leaving us?**, for example **Missing features I need** or **Shutting down the company/project**.
3. Type `cancel my subscription` to confirm.
4. Click **Cancel subscription**. The button is available only after you type the confirmation text.
</Steps>

To keep the tenant, click **Discard**.
