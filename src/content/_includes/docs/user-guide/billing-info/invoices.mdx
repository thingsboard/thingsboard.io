The **Invoices** and **Upcoming invoice** tabs in <DocLink product={props.product} path='user-guide/billing-info'>Plan and billing</DocLink> show past invoices and a preview of the next one.

Each billing period is paid in advance: the invoice is created on the first day of the period.

## Invoices

The **Invoices** tab lists all invoices, newest first. Each row shows:

- **Date** — the invoice date;
- **Amount** and currency;
- the status, for example **Paid** or **Void**;
- **Invoice Number**;
- **Payment type**, for example **Automatic payment** or **Manual payment**.

Two buttons at the end of the row:

- **Invoice PDF** downloads the invoice as a PDF file;
- **Invoice Page** opens the invoice page in a new tab.

## Upcoming Invoice

The **Upcoming invoice** tab previews the next invoice: the total amount (**Upcoming invoice for**) and the billing date (**Will be billed on**).

The table lists the billing period and a line for each item, for example **ThingsBoard Cloud** for the plan and **Edge Computing** for an add-on, with **QTY**, **UNIT PRICE**, and **AMOUNT**. Below the table are **Subtotal**, **Total**, and **Amount due**.
