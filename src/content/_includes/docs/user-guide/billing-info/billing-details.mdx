The **Billing information** tab in <DocLink product={props.product} path='user-guide/billing-info'>Plan and billing</DocLink> has two cards: **Payment method** and **Billing information**.

<Aside type="note">
  Card data is processed and stored by [Stripe](https://stripe.com/). ThingsBoard never has access to your full card details.
</Aside>

## Payment Method

The payment method is a credit or debit card: Visa, Mastercard, or American Express. The **Payment method** card shows the saved card (**NO PAYMENT METHOD PROVIDED** if there is none).

<Steps>
1. Click **Add** in the **Payment method** card. The **Add payment method** dialog opens.
2. Enter the **Card Holder Name** and select the **Country**.
3. In **Credit or debit card**, enter the card number, the expiry date (**MM / YY**), and the **CVC**.
4. Click **Add**.
</Steps>

## Billing Information

Stripe uses these details to generate invoices. The card shows the billing email and address, or **NO BILLING ADDRESS PROVIDED**.

<Steps>
1. Click **Update** in the **Billing information** card. The **Update billing information** dialog opens.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Company** and **Email** (required);
   - **Phone** (optional), in E.164 format, for example `+12015550123`;
   - **Billing address**: **Country**, **City**, **Zip / Postal Code**, and **Address** (required), **State / Province** and **Address 2** (optional).
3. Click **Update**.
</Steps>

Until the billing details are complete, **Plan and billing** shows the banner *Your billing information is incomplete*.
