The **AI Assistant** is a conversational AI agent built into ThingsBoard for working with <DocLink product={props.product} path="user-guide/calculated-fields" target="_blank">Calculated Fields</DocLink> and <DocLink product={props.product} path="user-guide/alarm-rules" target="_blank">Alarm Rules</DocLink>. Instead of filling out configuration forms, you describe what you want in plain language — the way you'd explain a task to a colleague — and the Assistant builds it for you.

Think of it as a ThingsBoard expert sitting next to you: it knows both features inside out, it can see the devices, assets, and data you actually have, and every change to your tenant is yours to approve first.

## Where to find it

Click the **Configure with AI** button on the **Calculated fields** page or the **Alarm rules** page. The Assistant opens in a panel on the right side of the screen, alongside the page rather than over it, so the chat and your configuration are visible at the same time.

## Using the Assistant

You can work with the Assistant in different ways, from asking a quick question to handing over an entire task. The more you hand over, the less ThingsBoard expertise it asks of you.

### Ask questions, get expert answers

The Assistant has detailed knowledge of Calculated Fields and Alarm Rules, so you don't need to know either feature to use it. Describe what you want to achieve in plain terms — even if you're not sure whether ThingsBoard can do it, or which feature fits — and the Assistant tells you whether it's possible and how, or what the nearest alternative is:

> _"I want to know when one pump is drawing more power than the others on the same line — can ThingsBoard detect that, and how?"_

Because it can also see your data and configuration, the same kind of question can be about your own setup, not just the feature in general:

> _"The High Temperature alarm on my Chiller profile never triggers, even though the temperature clearly goes above the threshold — what's wrong?"_

### Describe the outcome, let it build

When you're ready to hand the task over, describe the outcome and let the Assistant build it. You don't choose field types or condition modes, or write scripts — you state what you want to end up with, and the Assistant designs and creates whatever combination of Calculated Fields and Alarm Rules the outcome requires, picking the mechanics and writing any logic for you, including <DocLink product={props.product} path="user-guide/tbel" target="_blank">TBEL</DocLink> scripts.

> _"On each floor's meter I collect raw water-flow readings. Derive hourly and daily consumption per floor, roll them up into a building-wide total, and raise a warning alarm on any floor whose daily use runs more than 50% above its weekly average."_

It revises and removes existing configurations the same way — describe the change and the Assistant works out what to touch:

> _"We're switching the energy meters from kWh to MWh. Update the consumption calculated fields to the new unit, and adjust every alarm rule whose threshold depends on them so the limits still mean the same thing."_

Every change is proposed for your approval before anything is applied — see [Approval before changes](#approval-before-changes).

## How the Assistant works

### Context awareness

The Assistant is contextually aware. It can look up your devices, assets, profiles, and customers; check which telemetry and attribute keys an entity actually reports; and trace relations between entities. In practice this means:

- You can say _"my pumps"_ instead of listing device IDs — the Assistant finds them.
- It verifies that the data keys a configuration depends on actually exist before proposing it, instead of guessing key names.
- It catches mismatches early: if you ask to average temperature across sensors in a building, but no relations connect the sensors to the building, it tells you what's missing.

### Approval before changes

The Assistant never modifies your tenant on its own. Read-only operations — searching entities, reading configurations, and answering questions — run without interruption. Any action that changes something — creating, updating, or deleting a calculated field or alarm rule, or recalculating historical data — pauses for your approval first.

Before acting, the Assistant states its intent in the chat and shows an approval card naming the action — for example, creating a new calculated field or updating a specific alarm rule. The card identifies what will be done and to which entity. No change is applied until you click **Approve**.

## How to get the best results

The Assistant asks clarifying questions when something important is missing, so vague requests still work. A well-formed request, though, reaches the result in fewer steps. A few things that help:

**Identify the entity you mean.** The Assistant can search your tenant and resolve an entity from clues — a name, a type, a profile, or a location — so you don't need an exact match. The more clearly you point to the target, though, the less it has to search or ask back. And when the entity is already clear from the conversation, you can leave it out.

> Instead of: _"Create a temperature alarm."_
>
> Try: _"Create a temperature alarm for the Cold Storage device profile."_

**Say what you want, not how to do it.** State the result you're after rather than the steps to get there. If you prescribe a specific mechanism, the Assistant may follow it even when a better fit exists — so describe the goal and let it choose the approach.

> _"I want to know how full each storage tank is as a percentage, not just its raw level reading."_

**Include the numbers that matter.** Thresholds, units, time windows, and severities are the details the Assistant cannot infer — providing them up front skips a round of questions.

> Instead of: _"Alert me when the freezer gets too warm."_
>
> Try: _"Raise a critical alarm when freezer temperature stays above -15 °C for more than 10 minutes; clear it below -18 °C."_

**Continue in the same chat.** Keep working on a topic in the chat you already have — it preserves the context the Assistant has built up, so you don't have to repeat yourself. Follow-ups can stay short: _"Now do the same for the Warehouse profile"_ or _"Lower that threshold to 80"_. Start a new chat for an unrelated task.

## Scope and limitations

- **Calculated Fields and Alarm Rules only.** The Assistant doesn't configure other parts of the platform — dashboards, device profiles, relations, rule chains, and so on. When a configuration needs a prerequisite outside its scope (for example, a missing relation between entities), it tells you what's needed so you can set it up, then continues from there.
- **Alarms are read-only.** The Assistant can find and inspect alarms raised by your rules, but acknowledging, clearing, and assigning alarms remain manual actions.
- **AI credits.** Like other AI features, Assistant requests consume AI credits from your subscription.
