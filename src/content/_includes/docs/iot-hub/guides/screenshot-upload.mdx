{/* Where screenshots are uploaded and how the gallery orders them. Shared by the
    guides whose items are uploaded through the creator portal; solution templates
    carry their images in solution.json instead and do not use this. */}

Upload them in the **Screenshots** step of the new-item and new-version wizards, or later from the **Screenshots** tab of the version drawer. The gallery leads with the preview image and then follows your screenshots in the order you arrange them, so the picture users clicked in from is still the first thing they see. Any image in it opens full screen when clicked.
