Open ThingsBoard in your browser using the URL configured for your deployment. What happens next depends on how you got your license:

- **You registered through the <DocLink product={Products.PE} path="installation/register-community-grant" target="_blank">Community Grant Program</DocLink>.** Enter the Community Grant license key you received during registration.
- **You didn't use the Community Grant Program.** License your instance as described in the <DocLink product={Products.PE} path={props.guidePath} target="_blank">{props.guideLabel}</DocLink>.

Then log in with your existing Community Edition credentials and check that your devices and dashboards are in place.
