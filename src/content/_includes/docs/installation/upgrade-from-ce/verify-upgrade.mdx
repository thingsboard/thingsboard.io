Log in with your existing Community Edition credentials and confirm that your devices, dashboards, and other data are available.

Then check the customer's view. Go to **Customers & users &#8702; Users &#8702; All**, find a customer user, and click **Login as Customer User**. You don't need that user's password. Check that the customer's devices, assets, and dashboards are visible. If customer users save attributes or telemetry or acknowledge alarms from dashboards, test those widgets. If you haven't restored write access yet, see [Restore Write Access for Customer Users](#restore-write-access-for-customer-users).
