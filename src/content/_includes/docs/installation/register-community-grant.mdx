Starting with ThingsBoard 4.4, Community Edition and Professional Edition are unified into a single ThingsBoard platform that requires a license key.

Existing ThingsBoard Community Edition deployments can receive a **free perpetual license key** through the **[Community Grant Program](/community-grant-program/)**. The grant allows you to continue running your existing deployment after upgrading to ThingsBoard 4.4.

Registration must be completed **before upgrading to ThingsBoard 4.4**. The registration process runs on your existing ThingsBoard 4.3 deployment and generates the license key that you will use during the upgrade.

<Aside type="caution" title="Important">
Before registering, upgrade your deployment to the **latest ThingsBoard 4.3 LTS patch release**. Earlier versions cannot generate the deployment report required for registration. Follow the <DocLink product={Products.CE} path="installation/upgrade-instructions" target="_blank">Community Edition upgrade instructions</DocLink> if you are not there yet.
</Aside>

Registration does not modify your current deployment or activate ThingsBoard 4.4. Your ThingsBoard 4.3 installation continues to operate normally until you decide to upgrade.

## Register for a Community Grant

Log in to your ThingsBoard 4.3 deployment as a **System Administrator**.

### Step 1. Start the registration

On the **Home** page, click **Get your Community Grant** on the **Version** card. The button appears only if your deployment is eligible.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/get-community-grant.png', alt: 'Version card on the Home page with the Get your Community Grant button', caption: 'Click "Get your Community Grant" on the Version card.' },
]} />

Review what the grant includes and click **Register this deployment**. The **License Portal** opens in a new tab.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/register-deployment.png', alt: 'Community Grant registration dialog with the Register this deployment button', caption: 'Review the Community Grant information and click "Register this deployment".' },
]} />

### Step 2. Create or sign in to your License Portal account

Sign up with Google, GitHub, or email and password — or click **Sign in** if you already have an account. The grant and its license key are issued to this account.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/create-license-portal-account.png', alt: 'License Portal Create your account page with Google, GitHub, and email sign-up options', caption: 'Create a License Portal account or sign in to an existing account.' },
]} />

### Step 3. Accept the grant terms

Select the checkbox to confirm that you are authorized to register the deployment and agree to the [Community Grant License Agreement](/legal/community-grant-license-agreement/), then click **Accept and continue**.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/accept-grant-terms.png', alt: 'License Portal Accept the grant terms page with the confirmation checkbox selected', caption: 'Accept the Community Grant terms to continue.' },
]} />

### Step 4. Get your Community Grant

The License Portal verifies your deployment automatically. On large databases this can take several minutes; you can close the page and come back later.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/verify-deployment.png', alt: 'License Portal Verifying your deployment page', caption: 'Wait while the License Portal verifies your deployment.' },
]} />

Most deployments get the grant immediately; some go **under review** first. No license key is issued until verification is complete.

When the grant is ready, the Portal shows the granted scale (devices, production servers, and edges) and your **License key**. Use **Show** or **Copy** to get the key. A copy is also emailed to you, and the grant stays in your Portal account.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/community-grant-ready.png', alt: 'License Portal Your Community Grant is ready page with the granted scale and the license key', caption: 'Review the granted scale and copy your license key.' },
]} />

<Aside type="caution" title="Important">
Keep the license key — you will need it to activate ThingsBoard 4.4 during the upgrade.
</Aside>

### Step 5. Return to ThingsBoard

Back in ThingsBoard 4.3, the dialog confirms that the deployment is registered. Click **Close**.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/deployment-registered.png', alt: 'Community Grant registration dialog showing This deployment is registered', caption: 'Confirm that the deployment has been registered.' },
]} />

The **Version** card now shows **Community Grant registered**. Nothing else is required — keep using 4.3 until you are ready to upgrade.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/community-grant-registered.png', alt: 'Version card on the Home page showing Community Grant registered', caption: 'Confirm the Community Grant registration on the Version card.' },
]} />

<Aside type="tip" title="No internet access?">
Register offline: on any connected machine, download the read-only reporting tool from the License Portal, run it against your deployment, and submit the encrypted report through the Portal.
</Aside>

## What happens after registration

Registering a deployment:

- does **not** upgrade ThingsBoard;
- does **not** restart or interrupt your current deployment;
- does **not** change the licensing of your existing ThingsBoard 4.3 installation;
- registers the deployment with the Community Grant Program;
- creates a Community Grant for the registered deployment;
- provides the license key required to activate ThingsBoard 4.4 during the upgrade.

After registration, follow the <DocLink product={Products.PE} path="installation/upgrade-from-ce" target="_blank">ThingsBoard 4.4 upgrade guide</DocLink> for your deployment and provide the Community Grant license key when requested.
