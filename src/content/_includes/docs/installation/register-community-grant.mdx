Starting with ThingsBoard 4.4, Community Edition and Professional Edition are unified into a single platform that requires a license key.

Register your existing Community Edition deployment for the **[Community Grant Program](/community-grant-program/)** to get a **free perpetual license key**. The key lets you upgrade to ThingsBoard 4.4 and keep your current deployment scale. 

Registration does not upgrade, restart or interrupt your current deployment.

<Aside type="caution" title="Upgrade required">
Registration for the Community Grant Program is available only in **ThingsBoard CE {CE_FULL_VER}**. Earlier versions cannot generate the required deployment report. If you use an older version, upgrade first by following the <DocLink product={Products.CE} path="installation/upgrade-instructions" target="_blank">Community Edition upgrade instructions</DocLink>.

Complete registration **before upgrading to ThingsBoard 4.4**.
</Aside>

## Register for a Community Grant

Log in to your ThingsBoard CE {CE_FULL_VER} deployment as a **System Administrator**.

### Step 1. Start registration

On the **Home** page, click **Get your Community Grant** on the **Version** card. The button appears only on eligible deployments.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/get-community-grant.png', alt: 'Version card on the Home page with the Get your Community Grant button', caption: 'Click "Get your Community Grant" on the Version card.' },
]} />

Review the grant details and click **Register this deployment**. The **License Portal** opens in a new tab.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/register-deployment.png', alt: 'Community Grant registration dialog with the Register this deployment button', caption: 'Review the Community Grant information and click "Register this deployment".' },
]} />

### Step 2. Create or sign in to your License Portal account

Sign up with Google, GitHub, or email and password, or click **Sign in** if you have an account. The grant and license key are issued to this account.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/create-license-portal-account.png', alt: 'License Portal Create your account page with Google, GitHub, and email sign-up options', caption: 'Create a License Portal account or sign in to an existing account.' },
]} />

### Step 3. Accept the grant terms

Select the checkbox to confirm that you are authorized to register the deployment and accept the [Community Grant License Agreement](/legal/community-grant-license-agreement/), then click **Accept and continue**.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/accept-grant-terms.png', alt: 'License Portal Accept the grant terms page with the confirmation checkbox selected', caption: 'Accept the Community Grant terms to continue.' },
]} />

### Step 4. Get your Community Grant

The License Portal verifies your deployment automatically. On large databases, this can take several minutes; you can close the page and return later.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/verify-deployment.png', alt: 'License Portal Verifying your deployment page', caption: 'Wait while the License Portal verifies your deployment.' },
]} />

Most deployments receive the grant immediately; some are placed **under review** first. The license key is issued only after verification.

When the grant is ready, the Portal shows the granted scale (devices, production servers, and edges) and your **License key**. Click **Show** or **Copy**. The key is also emailed to you and stored in your Portal account.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/community-grant-ready.png', alt: 'License Portal Your Community Grant is ready page with the granted scale and the license key', caption: 'Review the granted scale and copy your license key.' },
]} />

### Step 5. Return to ThingsBoard

Switch back to ThingsBoard. The dialog confirms that the deployment is registered. Click **Close**.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/deployment-registered.png', alt: 'Community Grant registration dialog showing This deployment is registered', caption: 'Confirm that the deployment has been registered.' },
]} />

The **Version** card now shows **Community Grant registered**. No further action is required until you upgrade.

<ImageGallery images={[
  { src: '/src/assets/images/docs/installation/community-grant/community-grant-registered.png', alt: 'Version card on the Home page showing Community Grant registered', caption: 'Confirm the Community Grant registration on the Version card.' },
]} />

<Aside type="tip" title="No internet access?">
Register offline: download the read-only reporting tool from the License Portal on any connected machine, run it against your deployment, and submit the encrypted report through the Portal.
</Aside>

## Next steps

When you are ready to upgrade, follow the <DocLink product={Products.PE} path="installation/upgrade-from-ce" target="_blank">ThingsBoard 4.4 upgrade guide</DocLink> and enter your Community Grant license key when prompted.
