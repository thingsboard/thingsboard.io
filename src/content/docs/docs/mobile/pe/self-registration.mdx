Self-registration allows new users to create accounts independently, directly through the mobile app. New users can sign up and access the platform with predefined permissions through a registration form.

The Tenant Administrator turns it on and configures it per bundle in <DocLink product={Products.PE} path="user-guide/mobile-app-center">Mobile apps</DocLink>: the sign-up form fields, the CAPTCHA, the customer group and menu new users get, and the dashboards they see first. For every setting, see <DocLink product={Products.PE} path="user-guide/mobile-app-center#self-registration" bold={false}>Self registration in Mobile Apps</DocLink>.
