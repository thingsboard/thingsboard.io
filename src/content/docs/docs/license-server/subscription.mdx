ThingsBoard offers two payment models: **subscriptions** (pay-as-you-go) and <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="perpetual/" bold={false} target="_blank">perpetual fallback licenses</DocLink>.

With a subscription, you pay for the time you use the license, with no per-message fees. This keeps upfront licensing costs low for startups and suits established businesses that build and ship IoT solutions.

## Available Plans

- **Non-commercial** — free, for education, research, and non-profit use.
- **Free** — free, to start exploring the platform.
- **Pilot** — for teams launching an IoT project.
- **Startup** — for defined long-term projects.
- **Business** — for scalable IoT growth.

Compare the plans on the <a href="/pricing/?product=thingsboard-pe" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pricing</a> page.

## Create a Subscription

Sign in to the License Portal (or register), or click **Get your license** on the <a href="/pricing/?product=thingsboard-pe" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pricing</a> page. Then:

<Steps>

1. Go to **Subscriptions** and click **Add Subscription** (**+**).
2. **Choose product** — select **ThingsBoard**.
3. **Choose subscription plan** — select a plan and review what it includes.
4. **Customize** — add production instances (for clustering and high availability), AI credits, Remote Agents, and the **Edge Computing** or **Trendz Analytics** add-ons. The **Calculation summary** shows the total price.
5. **Order summary** — review the order, accept the **ThingsBoard License Agreement**, and click **Create subscription**. For a paid plan, add a card if you have no saved payment method.
6. Copy the **license secret** shown after the subscription is created.

</Steps>

If your account has no billing information, the wizard adds a **Billing info** step before **Order summary** (free plans skip it). To add billing details in advance or pay by wire transfer, see <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="billing-info/" bold={false} target="_blank">Billing info</DocLink>.

<Aside type="caution" title="Important">
Keep the license secret private. You need it to activate ThingsBoard — enter it when the <DocLink product={Products.PE} path="installation/" bold={false} target="_blank">installation guide</DocLink> for your deployment asks for it.
</Aside>

## Instances

A subscription covers one production instance (server process) by default. To run a cluster, for example in a container-based setup, add production instances in the **Customize** step. All nodes use the same license secret. See <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="instance/" bold={false} target="_blank">Managing Instances</DocLink>.

## Manage a Subscription

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. If you cancel a subscription before it ends, the unused amount becomes <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="billing-info/#account-credit" bold={false} target="_blank">account credit</DocLink>.

To show canceled subscriptions, turn on **Display canceled subscriptions** on the **Subscriptions** page.
