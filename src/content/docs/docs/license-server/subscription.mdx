ThingsBoard Inc. provides customers with two payment models: **subscriptions** (pay-as-you-go) and <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="perpetual/" bold={false} target="_blank">perpetual fallback licenses</DocLink>.

**Pay-as-you-go** is a billing model in which you pay for a ThingsBoard license on a time basis, with no additional fees per message. It suits small companies and start-ups that want to minimize upfront licensing costs, as well as established businesses that are developing and releasing IoT solutions.

## Available Plans

- **Non-commercial** — free, for education, research, and non-profit use.
- **Free** — free, to start exploring the platform.
- **Pilot** — for teams launching an IoT project.
- **Startup** — for defined long-term projects.
- **Business** — for scalable IoT growth.

See <a href="/pricing/?product=thingsboard-pe" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pricing</a> to compare the plans.

## Create a Subscription

Sign in to the License Portal (or register), or click **Get your license** on the <a href="/pricing/?product=thingsboard-pe" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pricing</a> page. Then:

<Steps>

1. Go to **Subscriptions** and click **Add Subscription** (**+**).
2. **Choose product** — select **ThingsBoard**.
3. **Choose subscription plan** — select a plan and review what it includes.
4. **Customize** — add production instances (for clustering and high availability), AI credits, Remote Agents, and the **Edge Computing** or **Trendz Analytics** add-ons. The **Calculation summary** shows the total price.
5. **Billing info** — enter your company, tax ID, contact details, and billing address. Free plans skip this step.
6. **Order summary** — review the order, accept the **ThingsBoard License Agreement**, and click **Create subscription**. For a paid plan, add a card if your account has no payment method yet (payments are processed by <a href="https://stripe.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Stripe</a>; see <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="billing-info/" bold={false} target="_blank">Billing info</DocLink> for wire transfer).
7. Copy the **license secret** shown after the subscription is created.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Important">
Keep the license secret private — you need it to activate your ThingsBoard instance. Follow the <DocLink product={Products.PE} path="installation/" bold={false} target="_blank">installation guide</DocLink> for your deployment and provide the license secret when requested.
</Aside>

## Instances

By default, a subscription covers a single ThingsBoard production instance (server process). You can add more production instances to the same subscription in the **Customize** step. This allows launching multiple instances that use the same subscription credentials in one server cluster, which is useful for container-based setups.

The **Subscriptions** page shows the number of production and development instances for each subscription. See <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="instance/" bold={false} target="_blank">Managing instances</DocLink> for details.

## Manage a Subscription

Once you purchase a subscription, you can upgrade or downgrade your subscription plan at any time.

<Aside type="note">
If you cancel a subscription before it expires, Stripe keeps the balance. After a while (about an hour), a negative amount due (**Account credit**) with the remaining units appears in the <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="billing-info/" bold={false} target="_blank">Billing</DocLink> section of the License Portal. This sum is deducted from the total fee of your next purchase — a new plan or a perpetual license.
</Aside>

To see canceled subscriptions in the list, turn on **Display canceled subscriptions** on the **Subscriptions** page.
