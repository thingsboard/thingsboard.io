A **perpetual fallback license** is a one-time purchase that lets you use a specific version of ThingsBoard on your own infrastructure without an active subscription.

The license includes one year of software updates. After that, you can keep using the platform and purchase software updates for subsequent years at a discounted fee.

A perpetual license suits you if:

- your security policy requires an isolated, on-premises, or offline deployment;
- you prefer a one-time capital investment over recurring expenses;
- you need a license tailored to your deployment.

See <a href="/pricing/?product=thingsboard-pe&solution=perpetual" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pricing</a> for details.

## Purchase a Perpetual License

Sign in to the License Portal (or register). Then:

<Steps>

1. Go to **Perpetual licenses** and click **Add Perpetual License** (**+**).
2. **Choose product** — select **ThingsBoard**.
3. **Choose license package** — select a package and review what it includes.
4. **Customize** — add devices, production instances (for clustering and high availability), AI credits, Remote Agents, and the **Edge Computing**, **Trendz Analytics**, or **Offline Mode** add-ons. The **Calculation summary** shows the total price.
5. **Order summary** — review the licensee and the order, apply a coupon if you have one, accept the **ThingsBoard License Agreement**, and click **Create perpetual license**. Add a card if your account has no payment method yet (payments are processed by <a href="https://stripe.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Stripe</a>; see <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="billing-info/" bold={false} target="_blank">Billing info</DocLink> for wire transfer).

</Steps>

## Manage Perpetual Licenses

The **Perpetual licenses** page lists all your licenses with their package, number of production and development instances, status, and the end of the software updates period.

To see canceled licenses in the list, turn on **Display canceled subscriptions**.
