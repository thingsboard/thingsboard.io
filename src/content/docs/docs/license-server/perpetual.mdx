A **perpetual fallback license** is a one-time purchase that lets you run a specific ThingsBoard version on your own infrastructure indefinitely, without a subscription. It includes one year of software updates. After that, you can buy updates for each following year at a discounted fee.

Choose a perpetual license if:

- your security policy requires an isolated, on-premises, or offline deployment;
- you prefer a one-time capital expense to recurring fees;
- you need a license tailored to your deployment.

See the <a href="/pricing/?product=thingsboard-pe&solution=perpetual" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pricing</a> page for package details.

## Purchase a Perpetual License

Sign in to the License Portal (or register). Then:

<Steps>

1. Go to **Perpetual licenses** and click **Add Perpetual License** (**+**).
2. **Choose product** — select **ThingsBoard**.
3. **Choose license package** — select a package and review what it includes.
4. **Customize** — add devices, production instances (for clustering and high availability), AI credits, Remote Agents, and the **Edge Computing**, **Trendz Analytics**, or **Offline Mode** add-ons. The **Calculation summary** shows the total price.
5. **Order summary** — review the licensee details and the order, enter a coupon if you have one, accept the **ThingsBoard License Agreement**, and click **Create perpetual license**. Add a card if you have no saved payment method.
6. Copy the **license secret** shown after the license is created.

</Steps>

If your account has no billing information, the wizard adds a **Billing info** step before **Order summary**. To add billing details in advance or pay by wire transfer, see <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="billing-info/" bold={false} target="_blank">Billing info</DocLink>.

<Aside type="caution" title="Important">
Keep the license secret private. You need it to activate ThingsBoard — enter it when the <DocLink product={Products.PE} path="installation/" bold={false} target="_blank">installation guide</DocLink> for your deployment asks for it.
</Aside>

## Manage Perpetual Licenses

The **Perpetual licenses** page lists your licenses with their package, production and development instance counts, status, and the end of the software updates period.

To show canceled licenses, turn on **Display canceled subscriptions**.
