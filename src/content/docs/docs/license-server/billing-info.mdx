The **Billing** page of the License Portal has two tabs:

- **Billing information** — your payment method and the details printed on invoices.
- **Invoices** — invoices for all your subscriptions and perpetual licenses.

Card payments are processed by <a href="https://stripe.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Stripe</a>. ThingsBoard Inc. has no access to your card data.

## Payment Method

Pay with a Visa, Mastercard, or American Express credit or debit card. A saved card is charged automatically, with no extra confirmation.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Billing** → **Billing information** and click **Create** in the **Payment method** card.
2. Enter the **Card Holder Name**, select the **Country**, and enter the card number, expiry date, and CVC.
3. Click **Create**.

</Steps>

Without a saved card, the License Portal asks for one when you create a paid subscription or a perpetual license.

To pay by **wire transfer**, create a <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="subscription/" bold={false} target="_blank">subscription</DocLink> or a <DocLink product={Products.LICENSE} path="perpetual/" bold={false} target="_blank">perpetual license</DocLink> without adding a card. The license stays inactive until the invoice is paid.

## Billing Information

Stripe uses this information to generate invoices. You can update it at any time.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Billing** → **Billing information** and click **Update** in the **Billing information** card.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Company** and **Email** (required);
   - **Tax ID type** and **Tax ID** (optional);
   - **Phone** (optional), in E.164 format: `+` followed by the country code and number;
   - **Billing address**: **Country**, **City**, **Zip / Postal Code**, and **Address** (required), and **State / Province** (optional).
3. Click **Update**.

</Steps>

If your account has no billing information, the License Portal asks for it when you create a subscription or a perpetual license.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated automatically. The **Invoices** tab lists each one with its date, amount, status (for example, **Paid** or **Void**), invoice number, and payment type. For each invoice:

- **Invoice PDF** downloads the invoice as a PDF file;
- **Invoice Page** opens the invoice on Stripe, where you can view payment details and download the invoice and receipt.

## Account Credit

Payments are non-refundable. If you cancel a subscription before it ends, the unused amount is credited to your account. Within about an hour, an **Account credit** notice appears on the **Billing information** tab. The credit is deducted from your next purchase — a new plan or a perpetual license.

## Coupons

Administrators can provision coupons (promo codes). Partners, distributors, and marketing campaigns use them to offer discounts on subscriptions and perpetual licenses. Enter a coupon in the **Order summary** step.
