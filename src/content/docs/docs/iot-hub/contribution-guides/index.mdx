ThingsBoard IoT Hub is the marketplace where the ThingsBoard ecosystem discovers, installs, and builds upon your work. When you contribute a device package, solution template, widget, rule chain, calculated field, or alarm rule, it becomes available to thousands of ThingsBoard tenants worldwide — installable in one click.

## Why contribute?

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Hardware Partners" icon="server">
    **The "Plug-and-Play" Standard** — Turn your hardware into a complete IoT experience. IoT Hub provides a native, ready-to-use dashboard so customers can start visualizing device data in minutes.

    **Support Cost Reduction** — Automate setup and eliminate the majority of onboarding errors. One-click installation means fewer basic configuration support tickets.

    **Global Showcase** — Your devices become visible to hundreds of thousands of ThingsBoard tenants worldwide, positioned as the recommended hardware for the ecosystem.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Distributors" icon="external">
    **Value-Added Distribution** — Bundle hardware with high-level software templates, creating a product competitors can't easily replicate.

    **Dual Revenue Stream** — Add a passive income through our affiliate program — up to a 30% commission when users register in ThingsBoard via your component link.

    **Direct Inbound Channel** — Every asset you publish acts as a lead magnet. Users who install your dashboards can reach out to you directly for hardware procurement or systems integration via your Creator profile.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Creators & Affiliates" icon="pencil">
    **Professional Identity** — Position yourself as a trusted IoT expert in the global ThingsBoard community. Your Creator profile in IoT Hub works as a searchable professional portfolio for ThingsBoard users looking for proven solutions and expertise.

    **Monetize Expertise** — Turn your custom widgets, rule chains, and IoT solutions into new business opportunities. IoT Hub helps users discover your work and connect with you through referral and affiliate links.

    **Early-Mover Authority** — Become a Verified Creator at launch and get a stronger position in the IoT Hub ecosystem. Early contributors receive priority visibility in search results and may qualify for long-term featured partner status.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
