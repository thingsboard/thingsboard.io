A widget installs into your ThingsBoard **Widgets library** (Resources → Widgets library). Once installed, you can add it to any dashboard.

<Steps>
1. Open **IoT Hub** from the left sidebar and click **Widgets**.
2. Browse the catalog. Use the left-panel filters to narrow results by **Type** (Timeseries, Latest), **Category** (Cards & Info, Charts & Graphs, Controls, Gauges & Indicators, Input Forms, Video & Cameras), or **Use case**. Sort by **Most Installed** to find popular widgets.
3. Click a widget card to open its detail panel. Review the datasource requirements (attribute names and telemetry keys the widget expects), available widget settings, and any browser or hardware requirements listed.
4. Click **Install** — either directly on the card or from inside the detail panel.
5. A confirmation dialog appears. Click **Install** to confirm.
6. An **Installation Successful** message appears. Click **Open Widget** to navigate directly to the widget in your Widgets library, or close and continue browsing.
7. To use the installed widget on a dashboard: open any dashboard, enter edit mode, click **Add widget**, and find the widget in the library.
</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  Widgets are self-contained — all HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and assets are bundled within the widget. No additional dependencies to install.
</Aside>
