Solution templates are the fastest way to see a working IoT dashboard. They provision all the required entities and open a live dashboard in seconds.

A solution template provisions a complete IoT environment in one click — device profiles, pre-configured devices, dashboards, rule chains, customers, and users — all created automatically within your ThingsBoard tenant.

<Steps>
1. Open **IoT Hub** from the left sidebar and click **Solution Templates**.
2. Browse the catalog. Use the left-panel filters to narrow results by **Category** (Agriculture, Asset Management, Energy, Industrial, Monitoring, Operations, Smart City, SCADA) or **Use case**. Sort by **Most Installed** to see the most popular templates.
3. Optionally click a template card to open its detail panel and review the description, highlights, included components, and real-world use-case scenarios before installing.
4. Click **Install** — either directly on the card or from inside the detail panel.
5. A confirmation dialog appears. Click **Install** to confirm. The platform automatically creates and configures all resources within your tenant.
6. When installation completes, a **Solution template successfully installed** dialog opens with detailed solution instructions. Read through it — it includes:
   - A guide to the main dashboard and its available states
   - Pre-provisioned demo devices with their access tokens
   - CURL commands to simulate sensor data and verify the setup immediately
   - Pre-configured alarm rules and their thresholds
   - Pre-created customers and users with login credentials
   - A complete list of all entities created (roles, rule chains, device profiles, devices, dashboards, customers, users)
   - Edge computing setup steps, if the template supports ThingsBoard Edge
7. Click **Go to main dashboard** to open the installed dashboard.
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
  The pre-provisioned demo devices let you see data flowing immediately. Use the CURL commands in the solution instructions to simulate sensor readings and explore alarms and dashboard behavior before connecting real hardware.
</Aside>
