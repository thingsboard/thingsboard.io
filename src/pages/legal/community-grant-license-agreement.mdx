# ThingsBoard Community Grant License Agreement

**Revision Date:** September 15, 2026

It is entered into between **ThingsBoard, Inc.** ("Licensor", "we") and the organization that registers ("Licensee", "you") — together, the "Parties".

You accept this Agreement in the ThingsBoard License Portal when you register for the Program. The Portal records what you accepted and when, and keeps a copy of your Grant Record in your account.

> **How this Agreement is put together.** The **ThingsBoard License Agreement** ([current revision](/legal/license-agreement/)) sets out the general terms on which the Software is licensed — license keys and scope enforcement, telemetry, offline deployments, warranties, indemnity, liability, confidentiality, intellectual property, and general provisions. **Those terms apply to your Community Grant license, as modified by this Agreement.** Where the two differ, this Agreement governs.

## 1. Background

**1.1** Effective from Version 4.4, we unified the legacy ThingsBoard Community Edition and Professional Edition into a single platform (the "Software"), licensed under the Business Source License 1.1 with the Additional Use Grant set out in the [LICENSE file](https://github.com/thingsboard/thingsboard/blob/master/LICENSE). The separate Community Edition distribution is discontinued from Version 4.4 onward.

**1.2 Your Apache 2.0 rights are untouched.** Every release of Community Edition published before Version 4.4 remains licensed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0, irrevocably and in perpetuity**. Nothing in this Agreement alters, revokes or restricts those rights. Those releases continue to run under Apache 2.0 indefinitely, with or without further updates from us.

**1.3 Purpose of the Program.** The Program exists so that an organization already running Community Edition in production can move to the unified Software and keep operating at its existing scale, permanently and at no charge.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Grant Record"** means the record of your registration: the Granted Scope determined under clause 4, the instance identifier it applies to, and the date of registration. It forms part of this Agreement.
- **"Granted Scope"** means the Devices, Assets, Production Instances and Edges granted to you under clause 4, as stated in your Grant Record.
- **"Instance Check Report"** means the report produced by our verification tool when run against your deployment, as described in the License Portal Terms of Use.
- **"Legacy Instance"** means a self-hosted ThingsBoard Community Edition installation that was already running, under its own instance identifier, before the day we announced Version 4.4.
- **"Professional Pack"** means the Add-on described in clause 5.4, available only to Program participants.
- **"Registered Devices"** means the number of Devices in your Legacy Instance on the day we announced Version 4.4, as evidenced by your Instance Check Report.
- **"White-Labeling"** means modifying, hiding, suppressing, removing, replacing or overlaying the default ThingsBoard name, logo, copyright notice, product identity, or the visible "Powered by ThingsBoard" badge in the user interface or system outputs.

## 3. Eligibility and registration

**3.1 Who may register.** An organization operating a Legacy Instance may register that instance for the Program.

**3.2 Registration window.** Registration closes on **20 July 2027**, the end-of-life date for the Version 4.3.x LTS line. We will not accept registrations after that date.

**3.3 The Instance Check Report is always required.** Every registration requires submission of an unaltered Instance Check Report generated by the instance being registered. Its generation, contents and submission are governed by the License Portal Terms of Use. **Registration is not complete, and no Grant arises, until we have accepted a valid Report.**

**3.4 Verification.** We may verify any registration before accepting it, and may decline a registration we cannot verify. Submitting an Instance Check Report is your representation that it was generated by, and originates unchanged from, the instance named in it.

**3.5 One Grant per instance.** A Grant attaches to the instance identifier registered. Where an instance identifier is already registered to another account, we will tell you so and set out how to resolve it.

## 4. The Grant

**4.1 What we grant.** Subject to clause 3, we grant you a **perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, royalty-free license** to install, run and use the Software within your Granted Scope, at no charge, for as long as you comply with this Agreement.

**4.2 Including every later release.** The license covers Version 4.4 **and every later release of the Software, indefinitely, at no charge** within your Granted Scope. **No Update Fee is payable on your Granted Scope, ever.**

**4.3 How Granted Scope is calculated.**

- **Devices** — your Registered Devices plus a growth bonus of 10%, the bonus rounded up to the nearest ten. The bonus is never less than 10 Devices and never more than 1,000. **Every Grant carries a minimum of 100 Devices**, whatever the registered count.
- **Assets** — at parity with your granted Devices.
- **Production Instances** — as registered, with no bonus.
- **Edges** — as registered, with no bonus.

**4.4 Granted Scope is fixed.** Your Granted Scope is determined once, at registration, from your Registered Devices. It does not grow with your deployment. The 10% bonus in clause 4.3 is the only headroom the Grant provides.

**4.5 Operating your deployment for your users.** You may provide access to a deployment you operate to your employees, contractors, customers and other users, **including through the Software's multi-tenancy features and including as a hosted service**. Providing such access is not sublicensing, and you remain responsible for their use and for compliance with this Agreement.

This does not permit another legal entity to operate **its own** deployment under your license key. Distributing, reselling or embedding the Software for another entity to operate its own deployment requires a separate written agreement with us; reseller, distributor and OEM arrangements are available.

## 5. What the Grant includes, and what it does not

**5.1 Features.** Your Granted Scope includes **every feature of the former Community Edition, plus all security features of the Software** — including advanced role-based access control, Single Sign-On and OAuth2, and Secrets Storage, which were previously available only in Professional Edition. **They are granted to you at no charge.**

The remaining features are purchased through the Professional Pack (clause 5.4); additional Devices, Production Instances, Edges and Add-ons are purchased under clause 6.4. Clause 2.7 of the License Agreement applies: nothing is included unless granted or ordered.

**5.4 The Professional Pack.** The **Professional Pack** unlocks **all remaining features of the Software** — that is, everything not already granted to you under clause 5.1, **including White-Labeling**. It is available **only to Program participants**, at a flat fee of **USD 2,999**.

The Pack is defined by what it adds to clause 5.1, not by a list of named features, so it covers any capability of the Software however our editions change over time.

**Buying the Pack converts your license.** The Pack is a purchase, so buying it converts your Community Grant license into a **Perpetual Commercial License** under clause 6.1, governed by the License Agreement and the Order recording the purchase. Your Granted Scope comes with you at no charge (clause 6.2), our guarantees in clause 8 survive, and you may revert to the Grant at any time under clause 7.

The Pack does not change your Granted Scope: your Device, Production Instance and Edge counts under clause 4.3 are unaffected by it. It is **not time-limited** — you may purchase it at any time, including after the registration window in clause 3.2 has closed.

**5.5 White-Labeling.** **White-Labeling is not included in the Grant.** The default product branding and the visible "Powered by ThingsBoard" badge must remain unmodified unless you hold the **Professional Pack**, or your Order otherwise authorizes White-Labeling under clause 2.5 of the License Agreement.

**Buying scope does not unlock branding.** Purchasing additional Devices, Production Instances or Edges converts your license under clause 6.1, but it does not authorize White-Labeling. What you may use follows what you purchased — see clause 6.5.

**Deployments already rebranded.** This clause does not apply if your registered deployment was already rebranded on the day we announced Version 4.4 — in that case your existing branding may remain, at no charge, under the Grant. This permits you to keep the branding as it then stood; it does not grant access to the Software's White-Labeling features, which remain part of the Professional Pack. This paragraph applies only if your deployment was in fact already rebranded, and by relying on it you represent that it was. The permission survives conversion under clause 6 and reversion under clause 7, as clause 8.3 provides.

## 7. Reverting to the Grant

**7.1 You may always revert.** At any time, you may give up everything you purchased and return to your Granted Scope under this Agreement, at no charge. There is no penalty and no deadline. If you stop paying the Update Fee, you may continue on your then-current version, or revert under this clause 7 and resume receiving every later release at no charge within your Granted Scope.

**7.2 How.** Reduce your Devices, Production Instances and Edges to within your Granted Scope, and stop using the features that clause 5.1 does not grant — including restoring the default product branding, unless clause 5.5's exception applies to you. Tell us, and we will reissue your license key at Granted Scope. Clause 4.2 then applies again: every later release, at no charge, with no Update Fee. Your support reverts to Community Support under clause 5.2.

**7.3 This overrides the no-reduction rule.** Clause 4.7 of the License Agreement provides that a Perpetual License cannot be reduced. **That does not apply to a reversion under this clause 7.** Reverting to your Granted Scope is permitted however your license is otherwise characterised.

**7.5 Fees already paid are not refunded.** Reversion costs nothing, but clause 4.5 of the License Agreement continues to apply to amounts you have already paid.
