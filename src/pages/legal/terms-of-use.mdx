## 6. Community Grant registration and the Instance Check Report

Registration for the Community Grant Program must be processed through the Portal and strictly requires the submission of an unaltered Instance Check Report. You generate the report by running the verification tool against your own deployment; the tool operates locally under your control, opens the database read-only, and produces a single encrypted report. When you proceed with a grant registration, the tool uploads the report automatically. Where your deployment has no outbound connectivity, the tool cannot upload it, and you transfer the encrypted report and submit it through the Portal Yourself. Submission of a valid Instance Check Report is a mandatory condition precedent to receiving or maintaining a grant.

The Instance Check Report contains solely operational and structural metadata, including entity counts, creation-time histograms for devices and assets, server instance counts, database table sizes, system timestamps, technical identifiers of your deployment, two PostgreSQL server markers (the age of the database transaction counter and the server start time), and statistical checks that the deployment's recorded history is genuine. The Instance Check Report shall not contain, extract, or transmit telemetry data values, individual device names, dashboard contents, or personal data.

ThingsBoard logs and retains every Instance Check Report submitted, linked to the corresponding instance identifier, for the entire duration of the associated license or grant—including reports that do not result in a grant award. You acknowledge and agree that the Instance Check Report serves as the evidentiary basis for any perpetual no-charge license granted, and ThingsBoard reserves the right to retain such evidence for as long as the license remains active.

Generation of a Community Grant key additionally requires acceptance of the Community Grant License Agreement.

**Offline Deployments**. For deployments operating without internet connectivity, ThingsBoard makes available a read-only reporting tool downloadable exclusively via the Portal to execute against your local database. You shall not modify, reverse engineer, decompile, or tamper with the reporting tool, nor shall you generate, alter, or submit an Instance Check Report through any unauthorized or external means. Submitting an Instance Check Report, or proceeding with a grant registration that uploads one, constitutes your binding representation and warranty that the report was generated natively by, and originates unchanged from, the specific deployment named therein.
